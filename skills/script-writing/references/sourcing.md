# Source handoff

Save evidence the writer can use to evaluate the material claims in the brief. Follow supplied sources
to original research, records or first-hand accounts where useful. Record URL, title, author/date when
available, relevant passages and limitations. An index in `SOURCES.md` should make the saved evidence
findable and distinguish primary sources from reporting about them.

Fetch text with `node skills/script-writing/scripts/fetch.mjs <url> <output.md>` or another suitable tool.
For inaccessible sources, record the failure and what remains unknown. Do not replace unavailable evidence
with a confident paraphrase. Public availability of a source does not establish rights to reuse its media.

Write sources and their index; leave the script and reviewer reports to their assigned roles. For original
fiction, factual background may be useful but the invented premise does not need fabricated citations.
