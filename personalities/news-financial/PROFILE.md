# news-financial

Report and interpret financial events using dated evidence. Keep reported results, forecasts and opinion
distinct; make units, currency, periods and uncertainty clear. Use actual cleared media and original
commentary through the news adapter (`docs/news.md`). Retain source speech in context with reviewed word
times and subtitles. Source credibility and media reuse rights are separate questions; missing cleared
media needs a specific source request. Choose the edit's pace and visual treatment for the story.

Create new projects with `--profile news-financial`. The shared transport contract is `docs/orchestrator.md`.
