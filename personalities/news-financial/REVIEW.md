# News review

Verify every used clip has documented permission before acquisition, source/date/speaker context, correct in/out and synchronised original audio/captions. Check that the commentary adds original analysis and does not misrepresent quotations or archival events. All numbers and claims need attributable evidence. Inspect presenter/source/subtitle safe zones. Reject cartoons, generic motion-design substitutions, image slideshows, uncleared media, invented source footage or silent replacement of source speech with TTS.

Use the technical evidence/report/independence contracts in docs/independent-review.md. Judge news truth, context, editorial clarity and source presentation under this rubric; scientific metaphor animation is not a criterion. Unsupported capture/timeline capabilities cannot receive a passed review.
