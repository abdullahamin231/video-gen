# News review

Verify every used clip has documented permission before acquisition, source/date/speaker context, correct in/out and synchronised original audio/captions. Check that the commentary adds original analysis and does not misrepresent quotations or archival events. All numbers and claims need attributable evidence. Inspect presenter/source/subtitle safe zones. Reject cartoons, generic motion-design substitutions, unsupported presentation slides, uncleared media, invented source footage or silent replacement of source speech with TTS.

Use the technical evidence/report/independence contracts in docs/independent-review.md. Judge news truth, context, editorial clarity and source presentation under this rubric; scientific metaphor animation is not a criterion. Unsupported capture/timeline capabilities cannot receive a passed review.


Actual stills with commentary are valid. Inspect news.json, news-sources.json, permission evidence, source in/out,
assets/news-captions.json and the full assets/news-mix.wav. Every commentary word and retained source speaker must
have correct subtitles. Check speaker labels, quote context, offset alignment, dated reporting/opinion distinctions,
source credits, crop context and phone-safe legibility. Missing subtitles block acceptance. Review all audible
speech against transcripts; automated coverage/signal checks alone cannot prove transcription accuracy.

Before delivery require current review/news-verification.json for the encoded MP4: full-mix signal fidelity,
subtitle boundary/midpoint raster checks and media-offset raster checks, bound to exact source/video hashes.
Never describe software calibration fixtures or signal checks as a subjectively approved news film.
