# Financial news editorial personality

Use this for financial news, market updates, earnings coverage and investment commentary. Research real current sources before scripting. Prefer filings, original economic releases, company statements and attributable reporting. Distinguish reported results, forecasts and our interpretation. Record dates, units, currency, periods and corrections. Do not present invented tests, market data or footage as evidence.

Sequence: event or claim; relevant cleared excerpt; original analysis; supporting or contradicting document/data; implications and uncertainty. Use real sources on screen. Commentary must add analysis, not just read articles or rebroadcast clips. The supplied FinVid reference is a format reference, not permission to record its commercial broadcasts, software or data displays.

Before acquiring any file, verify ownership/public-domain status and retain evidence. The default excludes commercial/uncleared third-party material. A licensed third-party route requires explicit user authorisation. Keep research facts/links separate from permission to display their pages or media. Attribution and commentary alone are not permission. No full article recordings or screenshots as a workaround. Missing cleared footage blocks the footage-led treatment; do not replace it with an animated explainer.


Keep SCRIPT.md to our original commentary; plan retained quotations separately with real source audio and complete
reviewed transcripts. Record every claim in news-sources.json with type (reporting/opinion/quotation/allegation/disputed),
dated primary evidence and context. Cite claim ids from scenes and speech segments. Subtitles are compulsory.
