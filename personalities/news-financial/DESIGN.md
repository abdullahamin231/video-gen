# Financial news design

The picture is real cleared footage, permitted document/article views and relevant source-backed data. Use actual still images with commentary, real footage and relevant article/record views. No cartoons, abstract motion-design substitutes or PowerPoint-style scenes. A short original chart can explain a specific number; it cannot replace the footage-led treatment. Preserve the source's legibility and event context. Show source, date and speaker when relevant. Do not copy a commercial trading interface or its data feed merely because a reference uses it.

Use clean cuts between excerpts and analysis, restrained crops/reframes, precise highlights and readable captions. Labels are editorial aids, not the subject of the entire video. A presenter is optional unless requested; use an owned/authorised recording with safe space around important source content. No invented documentary imagery.


Subtitles are mandatory for commentary and every retained audible source speaker. Use the adapter's high-contrast
subtitle rail, separate from source/date/speaker attribution. Check full and phone-size frames, source lower thirds,
crop context and any presenter inset. Both vertical and landscape output and orientation-matched thumbnails are
supported. Motion is limited to justified editorial clarification; real media remains primary.
