# Financial news design

Use a composed broadcast treatment built from actual cleared event clips, relevant B-roll and original commentary. Show the company, people, operations or economic activity relevant to the sentence being spoken. Brief permitted articles, records, photographs and original charts support the report. No cartoons, illustrated metaphors, decorative diagrams, photo slideshows or sequences of number cards. The [FinVid reference](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dN1RHfd86F4) informs the format; do not record or download its commercial broadcasts, screens or data feeds.

## Editing and visual development

Open on the event or strongest supported contrast and establish the viewer's question immediately. Move between source evidence, our analysis and consequences. Choose cuts when the thought or useful visual information changes; return to relevant footage after a document or chart insert. Preserve meaningful continuous action and enough quotation context. Avoid long unchanged holds, repeated generic loops and arbitrary cuts or constant zooms.

Use deliberate changes of shot size, relevant cutaways, readable passage highlights and restrained reframing. A slow zoom or changing subtitles cannot make an unchanged data card carry a long passage. Keep stills and documents brief enough to support one point, with enough time to read it. Labels and headings guide the viewer; they should not become the picture for most of the film.

## Real data and purposeful animation

Animate original charts when they clarify verified data: reveal a comparator as it is mentioned, highlight the decisive observation and hold the result long enough to read. Use honest scales and visible source, period, units, currency and actual/forecast/adjusted labels. Then return to footage. Do not copy a commercial terminal or invent market values, intermediate quotes or price trajectories.

For example, reported and adjusted EPS are two measures for the same quarter. Reveal them beside one another with their definitions intact; do not animate one into the other as though the price changed over time. A historical certificate can establish archival context but cannot stand in for contemporary earnings activity.

## Composition, audio and captions

Use readable type, restrained original news graphics and clear source/date/speaker attribution. Keep credits, lower thirds and subtitles separate. Compose for the saved orientation: preserve relevant action and context in a vertical crop, or use an intentional inset when cropping would mislead. Avoid tiny landscape pictures floating in empty portrait space. Match thumbnails to the video's saved dimensions and check phone-size readability.

A presenter is optional; use only an actual owned/authorised recording and keep it clear of source content and captions. Distinguish original source speech from our commentary. Cleared natural sound may support the activity; speech must remain intelligible. Never invent documentary imagery, a speaker's words or their identity.

Subtitles are mandatory for all commentary and every retained audible source speaker, burned in with an SRT sidecar. Check sync, financial names/numbers, source lower thirds and presenter collisions in full and phone-size frames. A technically valid render dominated by long-held pages or data cards fails this visual treatment.
