# Rules-story review

Required: consistent near-black/white visual grammar with controlled red, yellow and green roles; sparse pictograms; the correct active rule; readable warning/captions; controlled narrator direction; logical escalation; a supported twist/payoff; stable objects and geography. Reject colourful explainer treatments, unnecessary slide cards, generic stock footage, copied branding, inconsistent rules and misleading factual framing. A deliberate hold or hard cut is valid here. Do not reject the film merely because it lacks the explainer's continuous physical animation or scientific causal diagram.

Use docs/independent-review.md for evidence, independence and report format; apply this rubric to visual-mechanism findings. Narrative consequence, recognisable rule/violation and suspense fulfil this genre's mechanism requirement. Profile compliance is a production requirement, not an optional taste preference. Prototype acceptance locks the look; later scenes must match it. Never infer listening quality from stills or transcripts.

## Frame-study and caption acceptance

The authoritative style evidence is summarised in docs/bradikinin-frame-study.md; production receives the written design principles, not the downloaded reference assets. Check soft-glow pictograms with restrained edge fringing, stable room relationships, purposeful yellow/green/red accents and large portrait framing. Reject a pristine generic vector deck or miniature landscape panel as a close style match. Exact source font/hex values are unknown; do not claim they were measured.

Inspect EVERY numbered rule introduction and demonstration. Require its number plus exact command, phrase captions aligned to the approved narration, matching negation/action/object and the correct active rule. Missing text, paraphrased instructions that change the meaning, premature captions, captions describing another action, unreadable text or collisions are blocking. A fully numbered caption can be composed as RULE + numeral tile + command. A large rule number alone is insufficient. Separate observed reference conventions from the user's stricter requirement that every rule be explicitly captioned.

Apply DESIGN.md's reusable asset/prototype acceptance contract to the current story's people, creatures, props and environments. Inspect unfiltered silhouettes and the required action/altered states, then finished close-ups and establishing views at delivery resolution and phone size. Unrecognisable subjects, inconsistent proportions, merged functional parts or unintentionally broken attachments are blocking. Require a reference-to-design table spanning multiple episodes with timestamps and declared implementation values. Assess drawing, framing, typography, captions, finish and state changes individually; palette compliance alone cannot establish visual resemblance. Judge whether the new story belongs to the same visual language, not whether it duplicates one reference episode's objects, plot or shots. Record missing reference evidence honestly. A user's rejected prototype requires a revised prototype and another user checkpoint before the remaining film proceeds.

## Title and thumbnail review

Review title and thumbnail as one package. Check that the title makes the everyday subject and impossible event concrete, uses a short direct instruction supported by the approved story, and preserves the story's actual negation and outcome. Check that the thumbnail has one dominant, original, recognisable anomaly at the matching feed size (180×320 for vertical, 320×180 for landscape, 320×320 for square), a readable uppercase command, and a clear visual relationship to the title. The title and image may share the command, but together they must communicate a specific mystery or danger without spoiling the reversal, inventing a rule, or overpromising the film. Reject generic horror art, clutter that hides the anomaly, unreadable copy, reference-derived artwork, and any claim that the pattern is a rigid formula. Record the chosen title, headline, description, click motivation and reviewer ranking in the normal thumbnail package.
