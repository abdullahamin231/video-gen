# rules-story

Create original uncanny fiction around a familiar situation. A rules-based treatment uses instructions,
violations and consequences to build tension; their number and order serve the story. Keep the geography,
recurring subjects and causal logic intelligible. Choose pacing, imagery and sound that sustain the intended
unease. A reference can guide tone without dictating a plot, shot template or copied assets. Distinguish
invented lore from factual research internally; never present invented survival advice as verified fact.

Create new projects with `--profile rules-story`. The shared transport contract is `docs/orchestrator.md`.
