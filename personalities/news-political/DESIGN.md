# Political news design

Use the actual cleared news/event footage and permitted articles/primary records with commentary. No cartoons, generic diagrams, standalone illustrative image scenes or PowerPoint-style scene sequences. Use clean cuts, deliberate pauses, relevant reframes and clear source attribution. Never conceal context with a crop.

Reserve a circular presenter at bottom left. Use our own presenter or a separately authorised identity. Size relative to the output format and keep clear of source captions, speaker names, subtitles and article text. Move the source content or presenter deliberately if a source lower-third occupies that area. A still portrait is not a lip-synchronised presenter. Never borrow a news host's face/voice as ours.

Keep the original source speaker audible when showing an excerpt and keep commentary distinct. Label archival material by date when it could be mistaken for current footage. Caption all audible speakers and preserve their meaning.
