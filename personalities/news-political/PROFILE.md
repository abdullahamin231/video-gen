# news-political

Explain political events using the primary record and attributable reporting. Distinguish established
facts, allegations, disputed claims, quotations and opinion. Preserve quote context, event dates and
archival labels. Use actual cleared media and original commentary through `docs/news.md`, with reviewed
source speech and subtitles. A perspective may guide analysis without distorting evidence. Missing real
media or reuse rights needs a specific blocker. The story determines the edit and presentation.

Create new projects with `--profile news-political`. The shared transport contract is `docs/orchestrator.md`.
