# app-review

Test the actual application before drawing conclusions. The recorded session is the evidence. Use
`docs/reshoot.md` to record the browser and `docs/capture.md` to ingest and edit it. Identify the build,
account capabilities and scope tested. Do not describe a flow the log does not contain. Keep failed and
unavailable results visible in the account. Separate observed behavior from inference and product claims.
Native desktop windows and OS dialogs outside the recorder are a capability boundary. Use graphics to
clarify the real session without fabricating states or concealing material omissions.

Create new projects with `--profile app-review`. The shared transport contract is `docs/orchestrator.md`.
