# App-review design

Actual app recordings and screenshots are the visual evidence. Use readable crops, purposeful zooms, cursor movement, click emphasis and small callouts. Preserve recognisable real UI; no drawn replacement of the application, generic diagrams or invented interaction states. Keep waiting/loading cuts honest and do not remove failed attempts that change the verdict.

Record action times, pointer positions, target bounds, scrolls and waits. Map the cursor and callouts through source trims, crops, scale and zoom. Never draw a second pointer over one baked into a recording. Keep source recordings unchanged and edit derived copies. A shot should show the action and resulting state, not just a slideshow of interface screenshots.
