Read on 20 October 2026 from YouTube's automatic captions of 16 uploads on
[Kai's channel](https://www.youtube.com/@kaiexplainsYT/videos), the same channel as the
[thumbnail study](kaiexplains-thumbnail-study.md) and [frame study](kaiexplains-frame-study.md). We read the first

Speaking rate across the full videos: 163–182 words a minute, median about 175. Videos run 10–22 minutes.

## The five moves

1. **A scene, not a topic.** Specific, physical details: cold coffee, a fan "trying to take off from a runway", four
   tabs open, "3 full minutes later, which is 170 seconds, the first word finally shows up".
2. **A reaction with personality.** "Two tokens per second, which is not even a speed so much as a medical condition."
   "I've microwaved meals faster than that." "My girlfriend walked in and asked what I was doing, and I said research,
   which she did not believe."
3. **What everyone believes, with its size.** "Threads at 2,700 upvotes and videos at 2 million views. And everyone is
   convinced that the labs nerfed their models."
4. **The turn.** "Now that explanation is clean and it is satisfying but it is also mostly wrong." "The box was never
   really the story."
5. **The promise.** "So today I want to show you exactly where it comes from, what it actually means for a daily
   workflow, and who should and who absolutely should not be spending their money on this right now."

The turn is a real one: the OpenRouter video's thumbnail says "OPENROUTER IS LYING", and about a minute in the video
says the popular quantization theory is "mostly wrong". The thumbnail voices the fear; the video settles it.

## What we changed because of it

- `script-writing/references/craft.md`: the cold open (these five moves) is the default opening for a long explainer;
  the narrator speaks as a person ("Be a person, not a reader").
- `references/tells.md`: a reaction that paints this situation is personality, not a tell; a turn against a belief
  people really hold is not an engineered twist. The lint no longer flags "picture this".
- `SKILL.md` and `references/review.md`: "I" is fine for opinions and reactions. "I did X" still needs the creator's
  notes, because inventing an experience misleads viewers. Without notes, the scene is the viewer's or a real person's
  from the sources. The script reviewer checks the cold open.

## Limits

One channel, automatic captions, the opening minute only. The repetition (the same "2 a.m." scene four times) suggests
a template; copying it literally would make our films sound alike. Nothing here measures which choices drive watch
time. Whether Kai's own first-person stories happened as told cannot be checked from the videos.
